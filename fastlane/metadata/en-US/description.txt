A desk day can pass before you notice how you're sitting. Take a short pause to pay attention to your posture and build a practice you can return to.

START SMALL
Free practice begins at three minutes. Wear compatible motion-capable AirPods, follow live alignment cues, and finish a sitting or standing session. Levels 1 and 2 are free, with a streak to mark your consistency.

NO AIRPODS WITH YOU?
Use a manual check-in to keep the awareness habit going. Live motion feedback and coaching require compatible AirPods; manual check-ins do not measure your alignment.

BUILD YOUR PRACTICE
The paid upgrade opens the full progression up to 15 minutes, posture walks, history and alignment trends. Optional all-day AirPods monitoring and Apple Watch nudges are there when you want them, but the daily practice can stay short.
