## Star prompt moments

Before: the only automatic asks were a good check-in and a streak milestone,
triggered from `AcknowledgmentView` and the tab switch back to Today. The
check-in is a daily reminder action, not the app's core loop, and the core loop
(the practice session) never asked.

| Moment | How often a typical user hits it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Passed practice session (completed, met the aligned-% target) | Daily, from their first session; level 1 asks for 50% aligned, so most completed sessions pass. Reaches almost every user in their 2nd or 3rd session. | The core daily action, finished with a good outcome, seen on the summary right before the ask. |
| Good check-in | Several a day for a reminder user, plus the manual check-in. | A clearly positive result. |
| Streak milestone (7, 14, 30, 60, 100 days) | Rare. Extra, never the only moment. | |

Kept as is: not before the 2nd cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped when the
call is made, opening the write-review page ends the passive asks, sending
feedback never does, never before onboarding and calibration finish. Settings
keeps its Rate row. The ask waits 3 seconds (was 3.5), then checks again that
Today is on screen with no sheet, cover, paywall or trial pitch over it.

Tightened: a session that ended on the trial pitch never records a moment (no
ask after a paywall). A moment blocked by a sheet used to wait forever and could
ask on any later tab switch; it now expires after 10 minutes. No launch, appear
or open-count trigger exists (the tab-switch path only resumes a fresh pending
moment).
